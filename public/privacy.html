PK Salary Tax privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026.

Who runs this

PK Salary Tax is run by Sheharyar Ahmed.

Data collected

The salary amount and period you ask about, sent to the connector by Claude when a tool runs. The connector does not ask for your name, account or other personal details, and it has no sign-in.

Usage and storage

The amount and period are used only to calculate the result and return it. The connector has no database, uses no cookies or analytics, and its code does not log these values. It does not read your Claude conversation, memory or files.

Third-party sharing

The connector does not sell or share your data. Vercel hosts the connector and processes technical request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect it, under Vercel's privacy policy. Your conversation with Claude is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

Retention

The connector keeps no inputs or results after responding. Vercel may keep request logs under its own policy.
